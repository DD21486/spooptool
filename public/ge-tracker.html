How to use GE Tracker

What items are on this page? We pull every Grand Exchange item that the OSRS Wiki tracks with real-time prices (sourced from RuneLite). Only items that have both a name/mapping and current instant-buy and instant-sell prices appear. After that, your filters (min margin %, min/max price, search) narrow the list, and Show top limits how many rows you see after sorting.

What to look for when flipping:

  • Margin % — Spread between buy and sell (after 1% GE tax). Higher can mean more profit per trade.
  • Limit/4h — How many you can buy every 4 hours. Low limit = less total profit even if margin is high.
  • Profit/4h — Estimated profit per 4 hours if you buy and sell at these prices up to the limit. Helps find the best gp/hour flips.

Columns: Buy (instant) = OSRS Wiki "high" — price to buy instantly. Sell (instant) = OSRS Wiki "low" — price you get when selling instantly. Volume (1h) = number of trades in the last hour. Prices are shown in full (no abbreviation). Margin % uses 1% GE tax on the sale. Click any column header to sort.

Chart: Use the Chart button on a row to see hourly high/low and margin over time. Helps spot trends and whether the current margin is typical.

Top opportunities: The table above the main list shows up to 20 "best quick flips" — items with positive net margin, profit/4h ≥ 100k gp, and either volume ≥ 50 trades/hour or margin ≥ 20k gp, ranked by return and liquidity.

Filtering & exclusion logic

Included in the list: Only items that have both a name (mapping) and current instant-buy and instant-sell prices from the wiki. Items with missing high or low are skipped.

Max price age: If either the last instant-buy time (highTime) or last instant-sell time (lowTime) is older than the selected "Max price age" (24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days), the item is hidden. This removes unrealistic margins from stale data (e.g. buy last traded 11 days ago, sell 3 hours ago).

Margin % cap: Rows with margin > 500% are always hidden; such values usually indicate bad or stale data rather than real flips.

Other filters: Min margin %, min/max price (gp), and search apply on top of the above. "Show top" then limits how many rows are displayed after sorting.
