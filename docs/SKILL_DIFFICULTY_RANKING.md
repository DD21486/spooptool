# OSRS Skill Difficulty Ranking (Hardest → Easiest to Train)

Ranking is by **how hard/long it is to train** to 99: slowest XP rates and highest time investment = hardest. "Easiest" means fast XP **and** relatively accessible (cheap or low effort). Based on OSRS Wiki XP rates, community guides, and common 99 time estimates (2024–2025).

**Cost rule:** Skills that are **fast but very expensive** (e.g. Prayer with dragon bones, Construction with mahogany, Herblore with best potions) are placed in the **middle tiers**, not the easiest. GP cost is treated as a real barrier, so "easy" = fast *and* affordable or low-effort.

**Note:** "Difficulty" is partly subjective. Some fast skills (e.g. Thieving) are very click-intensive; some slow ones (e.g. Fishing) are AFK. This list balances **time to 99**, **cost**, and **typical effort** (tedium, intensity).

---

## 1. Hardest (slowest / most tedious)

| Rank | Skill        | Why it's hard |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| 1    | **Runecraft**| Slowest in the game. Best methods ~30–65k XP/hr (bloods, souls, Ourania). No fast option; 200+ hours to 99. |
| 2    | **Slayer**   | Tied to combat and task RNG. Efficient rates ~30–70k XP/hr. Hundreds of hours; often trained alongside combat. |
| 3    | **Agility**  | Rooftops ~60–70k; Hallowed Sepulchre up to ~105k but demanding. Still 100+ hours of repetitive laps. |
| 4    | **Mining**   | MLM / Volcanic Mine ~50–90k. 3-tick granite is faster but very intensive. 100–200+ hours typical. |
| 5    | **Woodcutting** | Best ~60–90k (2-tick teaks, sulliusceps). AFK methods much slower. 100–150+ hours. |
| 6    | **Fishing**  | Barbarian fishing / infernal eels ~40–70k. Very AFK but slow; 150+ hours common. |
| 7    | **Smithing** | Without gold blast furnace, rates are slow. With gold (expensive), 300k+ possible. Mix of cost and speed. |
| 8    | **Defence**  | Same as Attack/Strength but often trained last; NMZ/crabs 100–400k depending on method. |
| 9    | **Attack**   | Combat; NMZ, ammonite crabs, etc. 100–400k/hr. Medium time, some AFK options. |
| 10   | **Strength** | Same as Attack; often trained first for DPS. 100–400k/hr. |
| 11   | **Hitpoints**| Trained with combat; no solo fast method. Goes up with Attack/Strength/Defence. |
| 12   | **Ranged**   | Similar to melee; chins for burst XP, otherwise 100–300k+. |
| 13   | **Magic**    | 50–60k with alch/splash until burst/barrage (slayer, MM2). Can be 100k+ with money. |
| 14   | **Farming**  | Time-gated (tree/fruit tree runs). Low *active* time but long calendar time to 99. |
| 15   | **Herblore** | Fast only with money (1M+ XP/hr with best potions). Slow and awkward without GP. |
| 16   | **Crafting** | Wide range: battlestaves, glass, d’hide. 100–300k+ depending on cost. |
| 17   | **Thieving** | High rate (blackjack, Ardy knights 200–300k+) but very click-intensive; many find it tedious. |
| 18   | **Hunter**   | Bird house runs + drift net / black chins. Mix of passive and active; 100–200k+ possible. |

---

## 2. Middle (fast but expensive – GP is the barrier)

| Rank | Skill            | Why it's middle tier |
|------|------------------|------------------------|
| 19   | **Construction** | 900k–1.1M XP/hr with mahogany tables/benches, but very expensive. Fast only if you can afford it. |
| 20   | **Prayer**       | 1–2M XP/hr on gilded altar with superior dragon bones; ~10–12 hours to 99. One of the costliest 99s. |

*(Herblore is in the hardest table above—fast only with GP, slow without—so cost places it conceptually in the middle tier; it’s not “easiest” because of the money barrier.)*

---

## 3. Easiest (fast and accessible – cheap or low effort)

| Rank | Skill          | Why it's easier |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 21   | **Firemaking** | Wintertodt 300–500k XP/hr, low effort, cheap. 35–45 hours to 99. |
| 22   | **Cooking**    | 800k+ XP/hr with sharks/anglers; AFK and cheap. 30–40 hours. |
| 23   | **Fletching**  | Darts 3–4M XP/hr; relatively cheap. Fastest skill; ~8 hours to 99. |

---

## One-line summary (hardest → easiest)

**Runecraft → Slayer → Agility → Mining → Woodcutting → Fishing → Smithing → Defence → Attack → Strength → Hitpoints → Ranged → Magic → Farming → Herblore → Crafting → Thieving → Hunter → Construction → Prayer → Firemaking → Cooking → Fletching**

---

## Sources / notes

- **XP rates:** [OSRS Wiki – Experience rate](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Experience_rate) (Cooking/Fletching 800k+; Runecraft/Agility/Slayer 20k–75k).
- **Time to 99:** Various guides (e.g. Gaming Elephant, Destructoid, TimeBusinessNews) and community consensus.
- **EHP / maxing:** Maxing all 23 skills is often quoted at ~2,300–3,700+ hours depending on efficiency; Runecraft, Slayer, Agility, and Mining are usually the biggest time sinks.

If you want to use this ranking in-app (e.g. to weight Skill Score by difficulty), the order above is a consistent reference.
