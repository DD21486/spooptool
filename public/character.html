Scoring methods

Your Boss Score is the sum of (kill count × points per kill) for each boss. Harder bosses are worth more points per kill. You also get +10 points for the first kill of each boss (once per boss).

Points per kill by boss (grouped by value):

Your Skill Score is the sum of points from all 23 skills (excluding Overall).

For each skill:

  • 15 points per level
  • +5 at level 40
  • +10 at level 50
  • +25 at level 60
  • +100 at level 70
  • +200 at level 80
  • +300 at level 90
  • +600 at level 93
  • +3,000 at level 99
  • +0.1 points per 10k XP (no cap; 13M ≈ +130, 200M = +2,000)
  • At 99: extra difficulty bonus (hardest skill +1,000 → easiest +300, scaled by training difficulty)
  • Total level bonus (stacked): for every 100 total level, +1 point added on top of the previous. 100 total = +1, 200 = +3 (1+2), 300 = +6 (1+2+3), 500 = +15, 1300 = +91, 2100 = +231.

Example: 99 Runecraft (hardest) with 13M XP = 6,375 + 1,000 (difficulty) = 7,375. 99 Fletching (easiest) = 6,375 + 300 = 6,675.
